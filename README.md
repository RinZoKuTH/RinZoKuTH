### About Me

I am a B.Sc. Mathematics & Computer Science student at Chulalongkorn University. Right now I am building an NLP FAQ chatbot for Chula on top of BERT and sentence embeddings, which mostly means a lot of data cleaning before any model gets to see anything. I competed in the initial rounds of AIHack Thailand and UNIHACK, where I usually end up on model training, backend architecture, and data engineering. On the side I work on the network layer of a peer-to-peer clipboard and file-transfer system, and I like writing combat systems and game logic in Roblox Studio and Pygame.
